What you get

The downloadable Signature Spell Check tool checks your documents right on your PC — Word-style: flags misspellings, suggests corrections, learns the words you add. It downloads in parts and assembles itself, the same way the Signature Antivirus download works. One download, then it runs on your machine whenever you need it.

  • spellcheck.py — the checker: point it at any text document.
  • words.txt — the full word list (downloads in 5 parts).
  • install.py — joins the parts and verifies everything.
  • Offline page — the whole checker as one file: double-click, no internet needed.

Then assemble:

python install.py --assemble
python spellcheck.py my-document.txt

install.py joins the parts into words.txt, checks every part arrived intact, and leaves you a ready-to-run tool. Nothing phones home — your documents never leave your PC.

Using the tool

python spellcheck.py notes.txt python spellcheck.py notes.txt --fix python spellcheck.py --add "myword"

--fix applies the top suggestion to each flagged word and writes a corrected copy. --add teaches it a new word permanently (your words are kept in my-words.txt beside the tool).
