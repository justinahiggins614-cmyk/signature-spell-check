✒ How to Use

Thirty seconds and you are checking like a pro.

🏠 Front Door ⬇ Download Tool 📖 How to Use 📚 Word Archive

1The word bar

Type one word, tap Check. Green means correct; red means it is not in the dictionary, with Did you mean suggestions — tap one to take it. If the word is right anyway (a name, a new term), tap Add to my dictionary and it is never flagged again.

2The document box

Type or paste your whole document. Misspellings get the red wavy underline as you type — tap any flagged word for suggestions, Ignore, or Add to dictionary.

Check Document walks you through every flagged word Word-style, one at a time: pick a suggestion, then Change, Change All, Ignore, Ignore All, or Add to Dictionary. Undo takes back the last change.

The AutoCorrect switch fixes classic typos (teh → the) the moment you hit space.

3It learns you

Three memories, all on this device only:

  • Your dictionary — words you add. View and remove them with 📖 My Dictionary; ⬇ Export saves them as a text file.
  • Your corrections — every suggestion you pick is remembered, and ranks first next time that word appears.
  • Your usage — words you type often rise in the suggestion ranking.

Away from users, a background job compiles the shared pattern pack from the Signature Dictionary and library — word frequencies and common misspelling pairs — so suggestions keep getting smarter for everyone. Personal words never leave the device.

4What it covers

Built to do everything the well-known spell checkers do for spelling:

  • Word-style: check-as-you-type underlines, suggestion lists, Ignore / Ignore All, Add to Dictionary, Change / Change All, step-through document check, AutoCorrect.
  • Docs-style: tap-a-flagged-word suggestions, personal dictionary.
  • Pro-style: a downloadable command-line tool that checks your files on your own PC.

It checks spelling — American English, from the Signature Dictionary's own word list. It does not grade grammar or style; that is a different tool.

5Where your words go next

The background compiler exports the vocabulary the system learns for the Spec Catalog: new specs draw on the words the network actually uses, and brand-new words can grow into word-patents (JAH-WORD drafts) through the word-patent pipeline. A word you teach the system can become an invention record.
